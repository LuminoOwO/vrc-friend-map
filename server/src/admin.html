This server's own VRChat login

Your own account only. The server keeps VRChat's live connection around the clock: invites go to your phone (UnifiedPush) and friends' locations are logged with your PC off. VRChat may end the session now and then; log in here again then. Logging in from a server's address can make VRChat ask for an email code.
